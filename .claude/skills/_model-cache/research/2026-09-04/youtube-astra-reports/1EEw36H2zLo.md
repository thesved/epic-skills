### Strengths of Astra reported
- **Writing**: Clean, concise output lacking typical AI clichés ("crisp, they're to the point, there's no AI-isms, there's no slop" at 02:05).
- **Computer Use**: Capable of multi-hour autonomous local application manipulation, demonstrated via 5 hours in Adobe Premiere Pro (02:25).
- **3D & Visualizations**: High capability in building historical simulations and 3D web experiences (02:58).
- **Aesthetics**: Strong standalone visual design sense and color choices (05:19).

### Weaknesses, failures, refusals, costs reported
- **Overcomplication ("Show Horse, not a workhorse")**: Tends to add unprompted bells and whistles, cluttering UI workflows (01:38, 04:16).
- **Intuitive Intent Misses**: Fails to capture the simplest user flow compared to Fable 5.1 (04:40, 05:28).
- **High Cost**: Positioned in the expensive tier above GPT-5.6 Sol ("if you can afford it" at 06:23).
- **Reasoning Friction**: Higher effort reasoning settings exacerbate over-engineering tendencies (04:16).

### How-to-get-the-max tips (effort, prompts, harness, settings)
- **Reasoning Tuning**: Keep reasoning effort restrained for design tasks to avoid UI clutter and extra fields (04:16).
- **Task Routing**: Use Astra for direct execution, writing, computer use automation, and 3D visualizations; avoid using it for complex end-to-end UX architectural decisions without strict prompting constraints (06:28).
